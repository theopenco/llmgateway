### What the numbers say

1. **Impressions are not the problem, clicks are.** 2.47M impressions at 1.8% CTR. The US alone is 999K impressions at 0.44% CTR. Most of that is `/timeline` (676K impressions, 0.31% CTR) and `docs.llmgateway.io/guides/cursor` (106K, 0.32%): pages that rank for broad "new AI models" and "cursor" queries but whose titles do not match what the searcher wants.
2. **Branded search is strong and converts; non-brand is thin.** "llm gateway" + "llmgateway" + "devpass" variants = ~15K clicks at 24–72% CTR. Outside the brand, the biggest click sources are `/blog/best-ai-coding-plans` (3,799 clicks), `/models/gemini-3.8-flash` (737), `/blog/kimi-k3-claude-code` (736), `/providers/atria` (658), `/models` (714).
3. **Three clusters have demand we rank for but do not answer well** (position 4–10, CTR under 2%): model release tracking ("llm ranking", "new ai models", "llm models list": ~27K impressions, 3% CTR), Kimi ("kimi status", "kimi k3 cursor", the `irm … install.ps1` installer query: 42K impressions, 1.2% CTR), and the gateway category itself ("llm api", "ai gateway", "what is llm gateway", "litellm", "openrouter alternatives": 87K impressions outside the brand term).
4. **Coding plans is the one non-brand topic that already works** (88 queries, 5.6K clicks, 16.8% CTR) and it sits on a single post. It deserves a hub.
5. **PostHog says the site converts visitors to accounts**: `/login` and `/dashboard` are top-5 paths and bounce is 26%. More qualified organic traffic will convert; the bottleneck is the top of the funnel.
6. **AI assistants already send 2K visitors** per 90 days. Answer-first pages (definition, verdict, table in the first screen) grow that channel for free.

## Expected effect

Conservative, from the numbers above: lifting the five worst high-impression pages (`/timeline`, Cursor guide, `/pricing`, OpenCode pricing, fees post: ~890K impressions combined at 0.3–1% CTR) to a 1.5% CTR is +8–10K clicks per quarter. Taking `best-ai-coding-plans` from position 6.8 to the top 3 on 159K impressions is +3–5K. The new category pages (A, G) and the LiteLLM/best-gateway posts target ~100K impressions where LLM Gateway ranks 8–14 with no page matching the intent. Together that is a realistic path from 44.6K to 65–70K organic clicks per quarter without new rankings, and the weekly/monthly posts compound after that.
