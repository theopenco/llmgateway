## Entry price and credit windows

The [z.ai plan overview](https://docs.z.ai/devpack/overview) lists plans starting at **$18/month**. Its [FAQ](https://docs.z.ai/devpack/faq) describes monthly and longer-term billing options. Higher tiers and current regional offers should be checked at [checkout](https://z.ai/subscribe).

The standard five-hour / weekly credit allowances are **2,000 / 10,000 for Lite**, **12,000 / 60,000 for Pro**, and **28,000 / 140,000 for Max**. Tokens, model multipliers, and MCP calls affect consumption. Both windows matter; a large weekly balance does not remove the shorter limit.

## Pick the catalog before comparing quotas

z.ai is designed around its GLM coding service and integrated coding tools. DevPass lets a single developer spend a monthly dollar allowance across its live coding catalog, with premium weekly fair-use limits. These are different units and different model sets. Check the [z.ai model guidance](https://docs.z.ai/devpack/overview) and [DevPass catalog](/coding-models), then compare a representative coding session.

## DevPass pricing and limits

DevPass costs **$29/month for Lite, $79 for Pro, or $179 for Max**, for one developer. As of September 27, these plans include $87, $237, and $537 of monthly model usage respectively, with separate premium weekly fair-use limits. Optional pay-as-you-go overflow costs extra.

**Plan changes start October 15, 2026:** new subscriptions begin at 2× the plan price in monthly usage; existing subscriptions move to 2× at their first renewal on or after that date. Daily caps, tighter premium weekly caps, and revised Reset Pass benefits take effect October 15, including within existing billing cycles. Read the [plan-change terms](/legal/terms#october-2026-plan-changes) before subscribing.

Compare the live [coding catalog](/coding-models) and [plan details](/pricing). Client support depends on the API and custom-endpoint features of the coding tool; a DevPass key does not unlock every feature of a third-party editor.
