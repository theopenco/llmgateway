Until now, buying credits meant paying by card. Some teams do not want to put a corporate card on file for a $50 top-up, and some simply prefer to pay in stablecoins. As of today, **credit top-ups accept crypto**.

## Where To Find It

Open **Top Up Credits** from your billing page, pick an amount, and select the checkout button below it — the one showing Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Crypto.

![The credits top-up dialog with the checkout button showing Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Crypto below the amount selection](/changelog/crypto-payments-topup.png)

That button hands you off to **Stripe Checkout**, where crypto is one of the payment options alongside cards and wallets. Pick it, pay, and you land back in the dashboard with the credits already applied. Everything downstream is unchanged: same pricing, same 5% platform fee, same invoice emailed after payment.
