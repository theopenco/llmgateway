The Free plan caps an organization at 5 team seats, 5 API keys, and 10 projects. Until now the only way past those limits was contacting sales. As of today, the **Pro plan** is back — self-serve, seat-based, and live on your billing settings.

Pro deliberately has the **same product features as Free**. You are not paying to unlock functionality; you are paying to scale past the free limits, with optional add-ons on top.

## What You Get

|                       | Free | Pro                                          |
| --------------------- | ---- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Seats**             | 5    | 3–500 at $15 per user per month              |
| **API keys**          | 5    | 1 included per seat, +$5/month per extra key |
| **Projects**          | 10   | 10 included, +$5/month per extra project     |
| **SSO/SAML**          | —    | $300/month add-on (up to 100 users)          |
| **SCIM provisioning** | —    | $200/month add-on, on top of SSO             |

Every seat includes one API key, so a 10-seat team gets 10 keys before the first extra-key charge. The plan starts at 3 seats ($45/month) and scales to 500 — beyond that, or for volume discounts, talk to us about **Enterprise**.

## Manage It From The New Plan Page

Your organization settings gained a dedicated **Plan** page. It opens with usage bars — seats, API keys, and projects, each against your current limit — then a configurator with a live monthly total and a plain-words summary of what you end up with before you confirm.

Mid-cycle changes are prorated: add seats today and you pay only for the remainder of the billing period. Cancelling keeps everything active until the period ends, then the organization returns to the Free limits.

The **SSO add-on** unlocks the same SAML single sign-on configuration Enterprise uses; the **SCIM add-on** adds directory provisioning on top of it. Both flip on from the same page, no sales call required — for teams up to 100 users. Larger than that, SSO means Enterprise.
