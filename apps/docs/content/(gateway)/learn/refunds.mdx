Recent purchases can be refunded from the billing history without contacting support. The refund goes back to the original payment method.

## Where to find it

Every paid charge in a billing history has a refund button. When the charge can be refunded the button is active; when it cannot, the button is disabled and its tooltip says why.

| Product     | Billing history                                                                                                 |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| LLM Gateway | [Transactions](/learn/transactions) in the organization settings                                                |
| DevPass     | **Billing history** on the **Billing** page of the [DevPass dashboard](https://devpass.llmgateway.io/dashboard) |
| Lounge      | **Billing history** on the [Lounge pricing page](https://lounge.llmgateway.io/pricing)                          |

## What can be refunded

| Purchase                                         | Window  | Conditions                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Credit top-up**                                | 14 days | Your most recent top-up, with less than 20% of its credits used                                    |
| **DevPass** start, renewal, or upgrade           | 14 days | Your latest plan payment, the plan still active, and less than 20% of its allowance used           |
| **Lounge membership** start, renewal, or upgrade | 14 days | Your latest membership payment, the membership still active, and less than 20% of its credits used |
| **[Reset Pass](/learn/reset-passes)**            | 7 days  | The pass has not been redeemed                                                                     |

Only the organization owner can request a refund, and each purchase can be refunded once.

<Callout type="warning">
	Refunding a plan or membership payment ends the subscription right away, not
	at the end of the billing period. The rest of the cycle's credits are lost,
	and using the plan again means subscribing from scratch. Refunding a top-up
	removes its credits from your balance. Refunds cannot be undone.
</Callout>

## Requesting a refund

1. Open the billing history for the product and find the charge.
2. Click **Request refund**.
3. Pick a reason. A short explanation is required when you choose **Other**.
4. Confirm with **Request refund**, or **Refund and cancel** for a plan payment.

## Why a refund is unavailable

| Message                                             | Meaning                               |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Refunds are available for 14 days after purchase    | The refund window has closed          |
| Only your most recent purchase can be self-refunded | A newer top-up or plan payment exists |
| More than 20% of these credits have been used       | Usage is above the refund threshold   |
| The plan for this payment is no longer active       | The plan or membership already ended  |
| Only the organization owner can request a refund    | Ask the owner to request it           |
| This Reset Pass has already been redeemed           | Redeemed passes cannot be returned    |
| This purchase has already been refunded             | The charge was refunded before        |

For anything these rules do not cover, contact support.
