Airside is where the companies behind the models run their listing on LLM Gateway. It uses an airport metaphor: LLM Gateway is the airport, developers are passengers, and providers are **carriers**. As a carrier you claim your provider, register your fleet of models, file your fares, and see the traffic that reaches you.

## Quick start

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign up with your company email

Create an account at [airside.llmgateway.io](https://airside.llmgateway.io). The domain of your verified email must match your provider's API endpoint or website to claim it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Claim or register your carrier

Claim an existing catalogue provider, or register a new one. The LLM Gateway team reviews every claim before the carrier goes live.

</Step>
<Step>

### Register your fleet and file fares

Add each model with its context size and capabilities, and file its per-token prices. Once a filing is approved, the model is routable as `<provider>/<model>`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Watch your traffic

The **Traffic** page shows requests, errors, tokens, and billed traffic per model. Invite teammates under **Crew** so your whole team can manage the listing.

</Step>
</Steps>
