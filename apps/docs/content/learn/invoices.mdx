Every payment gets a PDF invoice. It is emailed to your billing address when
the payment succeeds, and you can download it again at any time from
[**Transactions**](/learn/transactions) in your organization settings.

<ThemedImage alt="Invoices" basePath="/learn/invoices" />

## Downloading an invoice

1. Open **Settings → Transactions** in the dashboard.
2. Find the payment in the list.
3. Click **Invoice** on that row — the PDF downloads as
   `invoice-<transaction-id>.pdf`.

Refunds produce a **Credit note** instead of an invoice, downloaded the same
way. It shows the original purchase amount, the percentage refunded, and the
refunded total as a negative line.

<Callout type="info">
	Only **completed** payments with a positive amount have a document. Plan
	cancellations, plan endings, and free credit gifts have nothing to invoice, so
	those rows have no download button.
</Callout>

The invoice number is the transaction ID, so it matches the row you downloaded
it from.

## Putting company details on the invoice

Company name, address, tax ID and invoice notes come from your organization's
billing information, under **Settings → Billing**:

<ThemedImage
	alt="Billing Information"
	basePath="/learn/invoices-billing-details"
/>

| Field                   | Appears on the invoice as                   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Email Address**       | The address invoices are billed and sent to |
| **Company Name**        | The billed company                          |
| **Billing Address**     | The billing address block                   |
| **Tax ID / VAT Number** | Your tax identification number              |
| **Invoice Notes**       | A free-text footer (e.g. a PO number)       |

<Callout type="warning">
	Fill these in **before** you pay if you need them on the emailed copy — that
	one is generated at payment time. The download in Transactions always renders
	from your current details, so if something was missing or wrong, update it on
	the Billing page and download the invoice again.
</Callout>

## DevPass invoices

DevPass subscriptions are billed on their own workspace. Open
**Billing** in the [DevPass dashboard](https://devpass.llmgateway.io/dashboard)
and use the **Billing history** table — every charge, refund and plan change is
listed there with its own download button in the **Document** column.
