LLM Gateway sends three kinds of email. Only two of them can be turned off.

## Account email

Invoices and receipts, payment failures, subscription changes, password resets, email verification, and team or SSO invitations. These are part of running your account, so they carry no unsubscribe link. The only way to stop them is to close your account from the [dashboard](https://llmgateway.io/dashboard).

## Product tips and offers

Onboarding nudges, feature announcements, and occasional campaigns. Every one of these carries an unsubscribe link and a one-click unsubscribe header, so your mail client can opt you out without opening the message.

## Credit balance reminders

Emails sent when your balance drops below 20% or 5% of your last top-up, or when your credits have not been topped up in a while. These also carry an unsubscribe link.

<Callout type="info">
	Unsubscribing applies to the address the email was sent to, across every
	organization that mails it.
</Callout>

## Turning categories off for an organization

Owners and admins can switch either optional category off for a whole organization under **Organization → Preferences → Email Preferences** in the dashboard. This is independent of a per-address unsubscribe: either one stops the email.

## Usage and compliance alerts

Budget thresholds, model retirements, provider incidents, and compliance policy changes are controlled separately, per type, from the notifications panel in the dashboard. Organization-wide compliance alerts also have their own delivery settings under **Organization → Compliance**.

## Newsletter

The newsletter is a separate opt-in list you join from the website. Its unsubscribe link is managed by our email provider and is independent of everything above.
