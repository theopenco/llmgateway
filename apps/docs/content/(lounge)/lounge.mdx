Lounge is the chat app built on LLM Gateway. Pick any model from the catalogue, switch mid-conversation, and compare answers side by side. The same app includes studios for images, video, and speech, plus live voice calls, on the web at [lounge.llmgateway.io](https://lounge.llmgateway.io) and on iPhone and iPad.

## Quick start

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign in

Open [lounge.llmgateway.io](https://lounge.llmgateway.io) and sign in with your LLM Gateway account.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose a model

Use the model picker above the composer. **Auto Route** picks a provider for you based on availability and cost.

</Step>
<Step>

### Chat, compare, or create

Send a message, turn on **Comparison mode** to ask several models at once, or pick **Group**, **Image**, **Video**, **Audio**, or **Voice** in the sidebar.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get more with a membership

A [Lounge membership](/learn/chat-plans) gives you monthly credits worth more than you pay, so heavy chat usage costs less than pay-as-you-go.

</Step>
</Steps>
