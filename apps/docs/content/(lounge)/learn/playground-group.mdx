[Group Chat](https://lounge.llmgateway.io/group) brings a council of models into one shared discussion. Models take turns reading the conversation and challenging the previous argument. For independent answers in separate panels, use comparison mode in the main chat instead.

## Start a discussion

1. Add 2–5 models from the model picker. They speak in selection order.
2. Enter a topic or choose a suggested question.
3. Click **Start discussion**. The first model makes a case; the next offers a counterargument.
4. Follow the streamed replies in one thread. Each round runs for five turns.
5. Choose **Stop discussion** at any time, **Continue discussion** for another round, or **New discussion** to start over.

Each response uses credits from the selected billing context. Group discussions stay in the current page and are not saved to chat history; leaving or reloading the page clears the discussion.

## Questions worth debating

- **Decisions** — Explore tradeoffs before choosing an approach.
- **Brainstorming** — Ask models to challenge assumptions and suggest alternatives.
- **Research** — Surface competing explanations and questions to investigate. Verify factual claims independently.
