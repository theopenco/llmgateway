DevPass is a monthly subscription for developers who code with AI agents. Instead of topping up credits per request, you pay one flat price and get a monthly allowance that covers the models your tools call. Your DevPass key works anywhere an OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible key does, so you keep the tools you already use.

## Quick start

<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick a plan

Choose a plan on the [DevPass pricing page](https://devpass.llmgateway.io/pricing). Plans come in three tiers, **Lite**, **Pro**, and **Max**. Higher tiers raise the monthly allowance and the weekly cap on [premium models](/learn/model-categories).

</Step>
<Step>

### Copy your key

Open the [DevPass dashboard](https://devpass.llmgateway.io/dashboard). Rotate the key under **API Key** to reveal it once. The **Quick start** panel next to it then shows the exact setup for each supported tool.

</Step>
<Step>

### Point your tool at DevPass

For Claude Code, set two environment variables and run `claude` as usual:

```bash
export ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL=https://api.llmgateway.io
export ANTHROPIC_AUTH_TOKEN=your_devpass_key
claude
```

OpenAI-compatible tools use `OPENAI_BASE_URL=https://api.llmgateway.io/v1` and `OPENAI_API_KEY` instead. Every other tool has its own [setup guide](/guides).

</Step>
<Step>

### Watch your usage

Requests appear in [Coding Agents](/learn/coding-agents), broken down by tool. Allowance meters on the dashboard show what is left this month and this week.

</Step>
</Steps>
